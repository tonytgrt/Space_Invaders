### New Features
- Bullets and aliens drop to ground as debris
- Picking up alien bullets as debris can shoot them back to aliens
- Alien debris can be pushed sideways
- Pushing them to designated recycling areas can accumulate Recycle Points (RP)
- Earning 15 RP gives 1 extra life, 30 RP gives a power-up that makes the player ship bigger and enables it to fire faster
- Aliens will move and fire faster as it gets closer to player. So recycling the debris for enhancement is a good winning strategy
